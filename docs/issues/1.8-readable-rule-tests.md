# 1.8 Readable rule tests (proposed: shrunk to S)

**Size:** S · **Area:** `tests/`

## Goal

A few tests that state the rules in plain numbers, next to the trace tests that prove parity.

## What to build

`tests/test_rules.py`, using a small level built in the test (a flat floor of `grass` tiles and one wall), not `arena.json`:

- [ ] A ground jump rises and returns to the floor; its peak equals the sum of the per-tick speeds from `JUMP_SPEED` down in steps of `GRAVITY`
- [ ] A second jump works once in the air, a third does not
- [ ] Jumping while pressed against a wall pushes the player away at 6 px per tick
- [ ] Holding `fast_fall` reaches the ground sooner than not holding it
- [ ] A bullet takes 20 health; five kill; the survivor is `winner`
- [ ] A bullet disappears after 500 px, and when it hits a solid tile
- [ ] Holding `fire` shoots once every 15 ticks

Compute expected values from `gg_core/constants.py`; do not hard-code numbers that would hide a constant change.
