## Risks that changed

| Risk | Was | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| pygbag can't handle part of the client | Medium | Low to medium | Rendering, input and WebSocket all worked in Chrome. Audio, Safari, Firefox and mobile are untested |
| Runtime depends on a third-party CDN | not listed | Medium | The build loads Python and pygame-ce from `pygame-web.github.io` at page load unless self-hosted |
| Browser cannot reach an Agones game server | not listed | High until 4.9 is designed | HTTPS pages need `wss://`; Agones gives a bare IP and port |
| Behaviour drifts from v1 during the port | implicit | Low | Bit-for-bit parity tests on about 8,000 ticks |
| Asset licences block a public deploy | not listed | Unknown | Not verified yet (task 0.8) |
